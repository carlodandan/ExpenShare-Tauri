Household Budgeting,
Simplified & Offline.

ExpenShare is a lightweight, offline budget tracker for two people sharing household finances. Track gross income, net balances, extra budget rollovers, and target goals without relying on third-party cloud servers.

How It Works & Features

Everything you need for clean, transparent household accounting.

Monthly Dashboard

Supervise monthly gross income and net balance between two people. Log fixed utilities and repeatable expenses with instant calculations.

Total Dashboard

Analyze lifetime cash flow, review expense breakdowns with visual donut charts, and inspect sortable monthly performance histories.

Extra Budget

Track accumulated surpluses across months. Seamlessly draw from Extra Budget for unexpected expenses while transparently logging any shortfall.

Set Goals

Create long-term savings targets like a House & Lot or Emergency Fund with live circular progress rings linked into your monthly ledger.

Offline & Local SQLite

Your financial records stay on your device. Easily export and restore encrypted SQLite database backups at any time.

Reports & Exporting

Generate clean, printable PDF reports and raw CSV spreadsheets formatted for personal archiving and accounting.

Available for Desktop & Mobile

ExpenShare is built on Tauri 2.0 and Rust for high performance, tiny memory footprint, and native OS integration.
