ExpenShare is a lightweight budget tracker built for two people sharing one household. Log income, split expenses, and carry over savings — with every record kept on your device, never on someone else's server.

Download Latest Release View Source Code

How it works

Everything you need for clean, transparent household accounting.

Monthly dashboard

Track gross income and net balance between two people. Log fixed utilities and repeatable expenses with instant calculations.

Total dashboard

Review lifetime cash flow, break down spending with donut charts, and inspect sortable monthly performance history.

Extra budget

Track accumulated surplus across months. Draw from it for unexpected costs while logging any shortfall transparently.

Set goals

Create long-term targets like a house down payment or emergency fund, with live progress rings linked to your ledger.

Offline & local SQLite

Your financial records stay on your device. Export and restore encrypted database backups any time you need to.

Reports & exporting

Generate clean, printable PDF reports and raw CSV spreadsheets formatted for personal archiving and accounting.

Available for desktop & mobile

Built on Tauri 2.0 and Rust for high performance, a tiny memory footprint, and native OS integration.
